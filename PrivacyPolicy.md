When you play This Game, certain information may be collected automatically by us or by third-party partners (such as advertising networks) integrated into This Game. This may include:

- <b>Device Information:</b> Device model, operating system version, language setting, and screen resolution

- <b>Approximate Location:</b> Your IP address may be used to determine your approximate geographic region for the purpose of ad delivery

If you purchase the Ad-Free Upgrade, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple, Inc. (App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play). We do not receive, store, or process your payment information. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase was completed.

This Game is free to play and supported by advertisements served by third-party advertising networks. These networks may collect and use information about your device and usage to serve ads relevant to your interests.

Our advertising partners may use mobile advertising identifiers and similar technologies to collect data. Each partner operates under their own privacy policy. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any ad network whose ads appear in This Game.

Our primary advertising partner is Google LLC, operating through Google AdMob. Google may use the data described above to serve personalized or non-personalized ads.
For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, visit: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
To review Google's Privacy Policy, visit: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

<b>To opt out of interest-based advertising:</b>

- <b>Android:</b> Go to Settings > Google > Ads. On Android 12 and later, select "Delete advertising ID." On earlier versions, select "Opt out of Ads Personalization."
- <b>iOS:</b> Go to Settings > Privacy and Security > Tracking, and disable tracking for This Game.

Opting out of personalized advertising does not remove ads from This Game. You will continue to see advertisements, but they will be non-personalized -- meaning they will not be targeted based on your interests or activity across other apps.

<size=28><b>4. APP TRACKING TRANSPARENCY (iOS)</b></size>

On iOS devices running iOS 14.5 or later, This Game may request permission to access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. A system prompt will appear asking whether to allow This Game to track your activity. You may allow or deny this at any time through Settings > Privacy and Security > Tracking.

If you deny tracking, you will still see advertisements in This Game, but they will not be personalized based on activity across other apps or websites.
